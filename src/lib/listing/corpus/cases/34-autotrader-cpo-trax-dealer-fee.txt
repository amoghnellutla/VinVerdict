This 2024 Chevrolet Trax 2RS delivers compact SUV versatility with modern conveniences and reliable performance. Finished in gray with a striking jet black interior accented in red, this used model offers excellent value with engaging driving dynamics and practical features for everyday adventures.

- Apple CarPlay and Android Auto
- Heated front seats
- Power sliding glass sunroof with manual shade
- Adaptive cruise control with lane change alert
- Wireless charging
- Rear backup camera and park assist
- SiriusXM satellite radio
